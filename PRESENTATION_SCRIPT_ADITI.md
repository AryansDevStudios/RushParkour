## Part 2: Concept, Philosophy & Gameplay Demo (Aditi Speaks)
*(Step forward smoothly after Aryan introduces you.)*

> **Aditi:**  
> *"Thank you, Aryan!  
>  
> *Respected judges, our project's guiding motto is:  
> **'Patience, Precision, and Perseverance.'**  
>  
> *In today’s digital era, millions of school students spend hours passively playing mobile and computer games. Under the **NEP 2020 Skill Education vision**, our goal was to shift from being passive game consumers to active digital creators.  
>  
> *The game is set on floating geometric islands high in an open sky. The objective is straightforward: traverse the platforms from start to finish.  
> But here is the defining rule: **there are zero checkpoints**.  
>  
> *A single miscalculated jump resets you back to the origin. Instead of arbitrary frustration, every attempt teaches spatial awareness, kinetic timing, and mental resilience."*

---

### Live Gameplay & Camera Demonstration (Aditi)
*(Point to the screen or place your hands lightly on the keyboard to demonstrate.)*

> **Aditi:**  
> *"We intentionally engineered the controls so that anyone can pick up the game in seconds:  
> • **A and D** to move left and right along the course.  
> • **Spacebar** to jump across gaps.  
>  
> *You will notice something unique about our camera:  
> While the environment, lighting, and platforms are built in full 3D space, the camera is locked to a stabilized tracking plane. There is **no mouse camera rotation**. This eliminates confusing, dizzying angles and allows the player to focus 100% on timing and balance.  
>  
> *And if a player wants to inspect the path from another perspective, pressing the **P key** smoothly cycles between behind-the-character, front, and overhead viewpoints.*  
>  
> *Now, while the gameplay looks clean and intuitive, making high-fidelity 3D graphics and physics run at 60+ FPS on a standard laptop without lag required serious technical problem-solving.  
> Aryan will explain how we engineered this optimization."*

*(Turn to Aryan with an encouraging smile to pass the floor.)*

## Handling Judges' Questions (Your Domain)
*If a question is about the idea, why you made it, student learning, NEP 2020, or non-technical topics &rarr; **You shine here!***

* **Q: "Why did you choose a game instead of a normal software app?"**  
  * **Aditi:** *"Games represent the intersection of Art, Logic, Physics, and Storytelling—the core of the AVGC sector. It allowed us to explore multiple skills simultaneously: 3D design, logic programming, sound integration, and user interface design."*
* **Q: "Isn't a game without checkpoints too frustrating for students?"**  
  * **Aditi:** *"That is where our motto 'Patience, Precision, and Perseverance' comes in! Most modern games give instant gratification. Our game teaches that failure is simply a learning step toward mastery. In our student testing, players felt immense satisfaction when they finally cleared difficult jumps."*
* **Q: "How does this connect to NEP 2020?"**  
  * **Aditi:** *"NEP 2020 strongly advocates for vocational training and computational thinking. Instead of just learning theory, this project gave us hands-on experience in the growing digital entertainment and AVGC industry."*
* **Q: "What if a judge asks a deep coding/engine question to you?"**  
  * *Smile and hand it to Aryan:* *"Aryan engineered the core logic nodes and physics algorithms for that system—Aryan, could you walk the judges through that architecture?"*

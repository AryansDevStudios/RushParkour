Design Vision

Rush Parkour is designed to test your discipline and execution. With a complete lack of checkpoints, every jump matters. The game demands spatial judgment and absolute route mastery.

Built as an independent project, it demonstrates that world-class 3D software can be crafted by independent Indian developers using standard consumer hardware.

Expert players might clear the course in 4-6 attempts. Casual players? Expect 10-20 tries. It's built to be challenging, frustrating, and incredibly addictive.

The Fixed 2.5D Tracking Perspective

While Rush Parkour is set in a fully realized 3D world, camera movement is intentionally restricted to create a unique challenge.

  • No Mouse Control: The mouse is completely disabled by design.
  • Tracking Camera: The camera is locked to a stabilized tracking perspective.
  • Perspective Toggle: Press P to dynamically switch between Front, Behind, and Overhead views.

Game Mechanics

Precision engineering meets chaotic physics.

Sky Architecture

Navigate an open sky environment constructed from floating platforms, requiring precise trajectory planning.

Fixed-Plane Traversal

Master movement within restricted axes, forcing reliance on timing rather than camera manipulation.

Physics Triggers

Interact with a dynamic world powered by Chaos Physics Engine, where actions have immediate consequences.

AI Patrols

Evade NavMesh-driven enemies that patrol platforms and track your position. Contact means instant death.

Stomp Combat

Turn defense into offense by jumping precisely on enemy heads to eliminate them from the platform.

Ragdoll Death

Experience full skeletal mesh physics on failure, turning every mistake into a dramatic plummet.

Platform Types

Static Anchors

Stable ground. Your only safe havens.

Kinematic Movers

Timeline-driven platforms requiring perfect jump timing.

Reactive Collapsers

Triggered by physics on contact. Move fast, or fall with them.

Strict Controls

Mastery requires limitation. W and S are disabled. The mouse does nothing.

  • A / D Move left / right along the course
  • SPACE Jump
  • P Toggle camera perspective
  • ESC Pause menu
CRITICAL NOTE: There is absolutely no mouse camera control. The camera is a fixed/static tracking system. You must adapt to the perspective.
